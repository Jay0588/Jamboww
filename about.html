About the Company

Built in Mombasa since 1984.

Incorporated 1984

Jambo Wood Works Ltd began as a practical joinery and timber business. That practicality still shows in the work.

Based on Main Nyali Road along Fidel Odinga Road in Kongowea, the workshop serves domestic and commercial clients with joinery, soft and hardwood, building materials, hardware, and electrical materials.

The company operates both a wood workshop and a wholesale and retail outlet. That combination matters: the same team thinking about a cabinet detail also understands timber stock, hardware, paint, construction materials, and what will actually hold up on site.

Craft is not a slogan here. It is the sequence of small decisions before anything is installed.

Measure twice. Choose the right board. Route cleanly. Sand patiently. Check the reveal. Fit the hinge properly. Protect the client’s floor. Leave a finish that feels natural to touch.

01

Honest Materials

Hardwoods, softwoods, boards, hardware, and finishes are matched to the intended use instead of forced into one look.

02

Workshop Discipline

CNC routing and hand skills work together, giving repeatable precision and a human final finish.

03

Site Awareness

Good woodworking must arrive, fit, and survive the building it belongs to. Installation is treated as part of the craft.
