About Us

Family business.

Started in 1984, still in Kongowea, still the same family. We do joinery, hardware supply, CNC routing and paint tinting under one roof.

Our Story

Joinery and hardware,
done properly.

Jambo Wood Works Ltd was incorporated in 1984. We started as a joinery workshop and grew into a full wholesale and retail hardware outlet alongside it — so today, whether you need a wardrobe built or just a bag of cement nails, we've got it.

We work with homeowners, contractors, schools, developers and shops. Most of our customers have been coming back for years. We don't advertise much — the work speaks for itself.

We're on Main Nyali Road / Fidel Odinga Road, Kongowea, Mombasa — just before the entrance of Kongowea Market, opposite AON Minet Insurance and First Assurance Co Ltd.

2025 KNCCI Award Winners Best Use of Technology — Kenya National Chamber of Commerce & Industry
