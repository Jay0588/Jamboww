The Workshop

Joinery, timber, hardware, and installation under one practical roof.

Jambo Wood Works Ltd is a Mombasa family business serving homeowners, builders, designers, schools, and commercial clients. The work spans handmade furniture, doors, kitchens, wardrobes, CNC-cut panels, engraving, interior fit-outs, timber supply, and site visits.

Built around material honesty

Old-school workmanship, modern cutting precision, and a finish made for daily use.

Every job starts with the grain, the joinery, and the way the piece will live in the room. The team balances hand finishing with CNC accuracy so a carved door, a kitchen run, or a retail counter feels exact without losing warmth.

A grounded workflow from first call to final finish.

  1. 01

  2. 02

    Material Selection
