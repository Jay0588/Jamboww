About Us

Joinery and hardware,
done properly.

Jambo Wood Works Ltd has been running since 1984. We do all types of joinery — doors, windows, wardrobes, kitchens, office furniture — alongside a wholesale and retail hardware store.

We have a full wood workshop and a shop stocked with timber, paints, tiles, plumbing materials, construction chemicals, and more. Contractors, homeowners, schools, and developers all shop here.

Find us on Main Nyali Road / Fidel Odinga Road, Kongowea, Mombasa — just before Kongowea Market, opposite AON Minet Insurance and First Assurance.
